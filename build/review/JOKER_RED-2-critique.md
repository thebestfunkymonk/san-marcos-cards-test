# JOKER_RED pass 2 — senior critique (fresh eyes, 2026-09-24)

Reviewed: build/png/JOKER-RED.png, small/JOKER-RED.png, white/png/JOKER-RED.png, build/review/JOKER_RED-2-{pair,188-pair,1500,crop3x-*}.png
against brief §B, §C, §F.4, §H.17, §I, §J, style.md, ART_CONTRACT §3.3, and JOKER-BLACK (the Trickster).

Verdict: the construction is right (one Gill Red solid, paper knockouts, gold on paper, Aquifer eye only, QA clean),
but the drawing does not yet read as ONE pig diving. It reads as four islands: a rump with claws beyond the rim,
a bucket inside the ring, a gold crown, and a severed head below it.

## 1. Anatomy
1. **The head is severed.** The collar's paper ground (moat + closed V's, 20–35 px wide) cuts clean through the neck on
   both edges. The head is an island hanging under the bells, and its nape is scalloped by the bell bites. It reads as a
   pig's head on a platter or a mask. This is the worst problem: it breaks the one-silhouette rule and the dignity rule.
2. **The ear is not on the head.** It is rooted in the collar ground between body and head and stands clear of both, so it
   reads as a fin, leaf or horn. A prick ear springs from the poll behind the eye and, swept back, lies over the collar.
3. **The barrel is a bucket.** The ring's inner rim clips the barrel on a concentric arc; the back edge is nearly straight;
   the belly window has a straight edge. That makes a flowerpot outline. The barrel is also about 75 % of the ring's inner
   diameter, so the pig fills the porthole. He looks stuck in it rather than diving through it. The large shoulder
   arc across the barrel reads as a bucket rim or helmet edge, not as a shoulder.
4. **The hindquarters read as pliers.** The rump beyond the rim has two hind legs sticking out horizontally to the left,
   parallel, with pencil-point trotters. There is no thigh, hock and cannon read. The tail's long thin stalk looks like a hook
   or handle.
5. **The forelegs** are a stiff peg hanging down (near) and a boot (far). Neither says "front trotters forward" (§H.17), and
   the fore trotters do not break out below the ring.
6. **The trotters** are wedges with two paper dashes each. The cleft is a dash, not a cloven notch in the outline. At 188 they are
   noise.

## 2. Readability at 188 px
The snout disc and the eye are the only strong pig signals. The rest is a red blob in a gold ring, a detached head, and
claws. It is not yet "instantly a pig diving". What reads at 188 is outline: a continuous back arc from tail to snout, a
round ham, a cloven foot, a laid-back triangular ear, a curl of tail.

## 3. Dignity vs cartoon
The face is sober, which is good. But the severed-head read is macabre, and the splayed hind legs are comic (a pig kicking in
the air). The brief asks for full heraldic dignity: a heraldic pig volant, like a boar courant, not a flailing one.

## 4. Pairing with the Trickster
The Trickster is one continuous gesture from tail keel to bill, with one dense focal area of knockouts (the wing scallops)
and one hatched tract, and clean, calm edges. The Fool is fragmented, with sparse and scattered knockouts (eight trotter
dashes, a lonely mouth line), and hatch windows that read as vent grilles. It needs one gesture line
(tail → back → poll → snout) and knockouts that describe form (shoulder, elbow, ham, stifle, jowl), grouped rather than
sprinkled.

## 6. Knockout craft
MEDIUM 3.1 widths, rims and bridges are clean (QA 12 ✓). The problems are design problems. There are too many short
orphan dashes (trotters, mouth). The shoulder line is oversized. The belly window's straight edge and horizontal hatch read
as a grille. The ear window is lost inside a detached ear.

## 7. Gold on red (§C rule 4)
Satisfied: no gold touches red, and Aquifer is kept to the eye. But the paper ground is far larger than the rule needs. A 3.6 px
paper moat around each gold shape keeps gold on paper and lets the red neck run between the dags, so the head stays attached.

## Rulings on the redraw artist's departures
- **Dive angle 61°:** accepted. "Head-down on a slight diagonal" means a slight diagonal off the vertical, and 29° off vertical is
  within it. Keep the body between 58° and 66°.
- **Collar on paper, no Aquifer contour:** accepted, but only as a thin moat round the gold. No ground band.
- **Trotters without hatch:** to be retried on larger, properly cloven trotters (hatch one claw). If the window cannot hold
  two FINE lines with 3 px bridges, the departure stands, and the cleft must then be a notch in the outline.

## Fix list (priority order)
1. Reattach the head: collar gold on a 3.6 px moat only; red neck continuous between the dags; the nape unscalloped.
2. Root the ear on the poll behind the eye; lay it back over the collar; hatch its inner half.
3. Streamline the barrel. Make it slimmer relative to the ring (≤ 62 % of the inner Ø), with an arched back continuous into the
   neck and poll and a belly curving up into the flank. Keep paper between pig and rim inside the ring, so the porthole reads
   as a hoop he dives through.
4. Rebuild the legs heraldically: jointed (elbow, knee, fetlock; stifle, hock), cloven trotters with a notch in the outline.
   Both front trotters reach forward past the jaw and break out below the ring (one leading, one a step behind, knees bent).
   The hind legs trail together behind the ham, hocks flexed.
5. Reduce the knockouts to form lines: shoulder (scapula to elbow), ham (stifle curve), jowl, mouth, lid, disc rim +
   nostrils, trotter clefts. No orphan dashes. Belly tract only if it follows the belly's curve.
6. Shorten the tail stalk and tighten the curl.
7. Re-check at 188 beside the Trickster after every change.

Also changed: the head is drawn 1.1× for heraldic emphasis, the eye is a longer almond with the dot 3.1 px clear, and the breed is fixed as a Tamworth-type (a red, prick-eared, long-snouted bacon pig).

Rulings that stand:
- **Dive at 58°:** 58° at mid-barrel, 46° at the rump, 64° at the shoulders, with the head at about 53°. This counts as a slight diagonal off the vertical, with the head raised to keep the profile.
- **Collar:** it sits on a paper moat, with no Aquifer contour.
- **Trotters are not hatched:** a claw window after 3.6 px red rims is under 6 px, so it cannot hold a FINE hatch line with a 3 px bridge (§I.12). The cleft is cut in the outline instead.

Remaining weaknesses:
- The ring's face (about 34 px of paper) still separates the hindquarters from the barrel. At 188 px the rump and legs beyond the rim can read as a separate small mass.
- The barrel inside the ring is still the largest plain red field. It is heavier than the Trickster's body.
- The bells scallop the nape, so the collar reads partly as a crown-like ruff.
