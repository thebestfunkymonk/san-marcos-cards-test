# Curtis Jinkins playing-card style: research and rules to execute from

Scope: Monarchs (theory11, 2012) and Drifters (Dan & Dave, 2017), both illustrated by **Curtis Jinkins** (Neighborhood Studio).
Evidence tags: **[seen]** = observed in a downloaded reference image; **[measured]** = computed from a flat scan with numpy (scan DPI derived from the card's known 2.5 in width); **[read]** = stated by a source; **[inferred]** = my reasoning, not verified.
The reference images are in `research/refs/` (index at the end). They are for internal study only. Do not trace them and do not ship them.

---

## 0. Read this first: the style is not engraved filigree

The brief says "fine engraved-style linework, ornate symmetric filigree". The actual Jinkins look is different, and the difference matters:

- It is **monoline geometric line illustration**. Every stroke on a Monarchs back has the same weight (≈0.5 pt at poker size [measured]). No stroke swells or tapers like a burin line. There is no cross-hatching and no bank-note guilloche.
- The "engraved/premium" feel comes from four things. First, **density**. Second, **parallel straight-line hatching that fills exactly one half of a split shape** (half of a leaf, one side of a blade). Third, **Art Deco stepped frames**. Fourth, **production**: dark flood with reversed-out paper lines, metallic gold ink, and gold foil plus embossing on the tuck.
- The sensibility is **vintage Americana and sign-painter branding**, not Victorian bank-note engraving. It draws on WPA posters, badges, seals, ribbons, arrows, shields and Deco geometry [read: 59 Parks interview, It's Nice That].
- Drifters is the same designer in a heavier **folk/tattoo/linocut** mode. Line weight is about 2× Monarchs, with stipple-dot fields, rope borders and tapered flourish spurs.

If the San Marcos deck uses variable-width engraving it will read as "Victorian/bank-note", not as Jinkins. Use the monoline system below. Save any richness for density, hatching and foil.

## 1. Attribution and production facts

**The designer**
- **Curtis Jinkins**. Retailers often misspell the name as "Jenkins" (PlayingCardDecks, RarePlayingCards, X-Decks, Kardify) [read].
- Studio: **Neighborhood Studio** [read].
- Background: a Texas native based in the Hill Country outside **Austin**, roughly 30 mi from San Marcos, which is a nice provenance link. He previously worked at Planet Propaganda (Madison, WI). ## 2. Card-back anatomy

### 2a. Monarchs back
Measurements come from the Purple Monarch scan `monarchs_wopc_back.jpg`, 1496×2108 px ≈ 600 dpi.

- **Layering.** One flood ink (navy or purple). All art is **reversed out to paper white** [seen]. Nothing is printed in a second ink on the back.
- **Coverage.** The flood covers **≈81 %** of the printed area, so lines take ≈19 % [measured].
- **White border.** Unprinted paper margin of ≈0.10–0.15 in, about **3–3.5 mm**. Sides ≈0.10–0.13 in, top and bottom ≈0.14–0.15 in; the scan is slightly off-centre [measured].
- **Flood corners.** The flood's corner radius is tight: **≈0.055 in (1.4 mm)** [measured]. That is much smaller than the card corner radius of ≈3 mm, so the white border looks wider at the corners [seen].
- **Symmetry.**
  - The frame is mirror-symmetric on both axes (D2) [seen].
  - The central emblem is **180° rotational (C2), not mirrored** [seen]. The snake heads sit top-left and bottom-right. The "REGIS" banners are left-upright and right-inverted. The side motto reads bottom-to-top on the left and top-to-bottom on the right.
  - Result: the card is one-way-proof ("no defined top or bottom" [read]).
- **Frame structure**, reading from the flood edge inward [seen, measured]:
  1. The outer rule is inset ≈0.07 in (1.9 mm) from the flood edge. It forms **Art Deco stepped corners** with 3 offset steps, stacked L-brackets and a quarter-round cove.
  2. **Dotted-square beading** runs along the corner arms: 5 squares horizontally and 4 vertically, square side ≈2× line weight.
  3. **Piano-key bars** (rows of short parallel bars, ≈0.075 in tall) sit at the top-centre and bottom-centre. Horizontal bar stacks sit at the top and bottom of each side band.
  4. **Side bands** carry the Latin motto "DE DUOBUS MALIS, MINUS EST SEMPER ELIGENDUM". It is set in a tiny condensed geometric sans in widely tracked caps (cap height ≈0.8 mm, ≈4 pt type), flanked by em-dash rules. It runs vertically between two rules ≈0.033 in apart.
  5. **Fan/sunburst half-rosettes** (quarter-circle rays) sit at the midpoints of the long sides, attached to a stepped plinth.
  6. The inner cartouche is a single rule with **concave quarter-circle corners**.
- **Central emblem** [seen]:
  - Two snakes interlace in three loops, forming a vertical chain of "8"s, around a double-ended sword whose hilt shows at both top and bottom.
  - The snake bodies are two parallel contours with a **sawtooth (triangle) band** between them.
  - The sword blade and grip are **diagonally hatched**. The crossguard ends in **small open circles**.
  - Twelve leafy branches radiate outward. Each leaf is a **vesica** (two arcs) with a midrib, and **one half is filled with parallel hatching** while the other half is empty.
  - Two small ribbon banners hold one word each.
  - Interlace crossings use gaps: the under-line breaks with a gap ≈1.5–2× the line weight.
- **Density and negative space.** The emblem fills about 75 % of the cartouche width. Leaves are evenly spaced, about one leaf-width apart. The ground is never stippled or textured; it is a clean flat flood [seen].
- **Vocabulary present:** Deco steps, beading, piano keys, sunburst fans, ribbons, laurel/olive leaves, snakes, sword. No acanthus, no crowns (the crown lives on the ace and seal), no guilloche, no lattice.

### 2b. Drifters back
Measurements come from `drifters_rpc_drifters-2.jpg` at ≈258 dpi.

- **Layering.** One flood ink (blue #1B3B74 in the scan) with lines and dots reversed out. The flood covers **≈80 %** [measured].
- **White border.** ≈0.11 in (2.8 mm) on all sides [measured].
- **Symmetry.** 180° rotational [seen]. The arrows at top and bottom point toward the centre, and the skulls are rotated.
- **Frame:**
  - A single rule frame with rounded inner corners.
  - An inner band of **diagonal hatch strokes**, like a rope or twisted cord.
  - **Rosette flowers** in all 4 corners.
  - A **fletched arrow** on the vertical axis at the top and bottom.
- **Central emblem.** A snake forms an **infinity/figure-8** with a diamond-pattern body and ladder rungs. There is a skull in each loop. Daisies, lotus fans and leaf sprays fill the ground.
- **Ground.** A **stipple field of dots in regular rows** follows the contours [seen]. It supplies tone without halftone.
- **Line character.** Bolder than Monarchs. Many decorative strokes have **tapered, thorn-like spurs** (folk/tattoo flourish) [seen].

## 3. Line-weight system

| Element | Monarchs | Drifters |
|---|---|---|
| Base (and only) stroke on the back | **4–5 px @600 dpi ≈ 0.17–0.21 mm ≈ 0.5 pt**. Mode 4 px is 35 % of runs, 5 px is 25 % [measured] | **≈4 px @258 dpi ≈ 0.4 mm ≈ 1.1 pt** [measured] |
| Hatch gap | **≈10–11 px ≈ 0.45 mm (≈2.3× stroke)**; pitch ≈0.63 mm ≈3.3× stroke [measured] | Hatch and dot pitch ≈3× stroke [seen, approximate] |
| Contour vs detail | Identical. True monoline [measured] | Court contour ≈7 px ≈ 0.7 mm ≈ **2 pt**; interior ≈3–4 px ≈ **1 pt** (2:1) [measured] |
| Court frame rule | Thin navy rule, ≈1 pt [seen] | ≈2 pt dark-brown rule with **notched corners holding a dot** [seen, measured] |
| Caps and joins | Butt/flat ends at hatch-to-contour contacts; sharp points at leaf tips; round or open-circle terminals on sword guards [seen] | Rounded joins; tapered terminals on flourishes [seen] |
| Fills | Outline only on the back. Solid fills only for the ace's spade silhouette and small dots/squares [seen] | Solid fills for silhouettes, leaves, skull shadows and dots; flat colour fills on faces [seen] |
| Hatching | Straight parallel lines filling **one half** of a split shape; 45° on blades; perpendicular to the midrib on leaves. No cross-hatch [seen] | Diagonal hatch bands, curved "wood-grain" contour lines on garments, stipple dots [seen] |

**Recommended weights for the San Marcos deck** (in pt at poker trim size) [inferred]:
- Base line 0.5 pt, the Monarchs mode.
- Frame accent rules 1.0 pt.
- Court contours 1.5–2 pt, the Drifters mode, if the courts are custom.
- Dots and beads at 2–3× the base line.
- No stroke under 0.35 pt, especially for foil.

## 4. Court cards

**Monarchs** [seen + read]
- The courts are the **standard USPCC (Bicycle-pattern) two-headed half-figures**, unchanged in drawing and **recoloured only**:
  - yellow becomes metallic gold/tan (scan #A78569)
  - blue becomes near-black navy (#27232E)
  - red stays red (#A4192B)
- They sit in a thin dark-navy rectangular frame.
- The large corner suit pip stays inside the frame.
- Takeaway: premium through restraint. The courts stay classic and the custom energy goes to the back, ace, joker and tuck.

**Drifters** [seen]
- **Fully custom** two-headed half-figures with **180° rotational** mirroring. The split line is diagonal or horizontal, depending on the figure.
- The frame is a heavy rule with small **notched corner tabs holding dots**.
- The large suit pip sits top-left inside the frame, as in the standard pattern.
- Style: woodcut/linocut-like stylisation.
  - Faces use few lines: heavy-lidded almond eyes, straight nose ridge, simple mouth, moustaches drawn as flat shapes.
  - Hair is a set of parallel ochre ribbons.
- Garment fills are patterns rather than shading:
  - **columns of small suit pips** on lapels and bandoliers
  - dotted stitch lines
  - spiral or wood-grain line fills on red panels
  - paisley or arabesque squiggles in ochre on dark-brown panels
  - small four-petal flowers and stars
- Props: revolvers, sabres, Bowie knives, hats, cigarillos.
- Colour: dark-brown key line, **flat ochre (non-metallic)**, red and paper white. No gradients.
- The degree of customisation is high, but the composition stays close to tradition: two heads, a weapon or flower in hand, the large pip in the corner.

## 5. Pips, indices, aces, jokers

**Pip cards (both decks):** standard USPCC pip layouts [seen]. Drifters uses slightly custom suit silhouettes. Its spade has a broad top and a flared, tapered stem; its pip cards are printed in dark brown (#3B2D2F), not black [measured/seen].

**Indices**
- **Monarchs:** a **monoline slab serif** with geometric round "3", slab feet on "A" and a crossing-tail "Q"; medium weight [seen].
  - Rank cap height **≈0.35 in (≈9 mm, ≈10 % of card height)**.
  - Index pip ≈0.28 in tall.
  - Inset ≈0.10–0.12 in from the left edge [measured, ≈262 dpi scan].
  - Joker index: vertical stacked "JOKER" in the same slab.
- **Drifters:** a **condensed flat-sided grotesque**, industrial, with a squared "3" [seen].
  - Rank ≈0.26 in (≈6.7 mm) tall and ≈0.14 in wide.
  - Index pip ≈0.17 in.
  - Inset ≈0.14 in [measured, ≈258 dpi scan].
- Both decks use two corners only (top-left and bottom-right). Rank sits above pip, centred on a common axis [seen].

**Ace of Spades**
- **Monarchs** [seen]:
  - A large **solid dark spade silhouette**, ≈45 % of card width.
  - Inside it: a monoline **gold crown** with orb and cross, sunburst rays in the lobes, and a hatched stem.
  - Below it, in gold small caps: "TRADE — MARK" split around the stem, a bold condensed maker line, a "— MADE IN THE —" rule line, and "UNITED STATES OF AMERICA" on an **arched baseline**.
- **Drifters** [seen]:
  - A dark-brown spade with a thin **offset ochre keyline**.
  - Inside it: crossed revolvers, crossed keys, a skull, a flower and a star.
  - Above it: a ribbon reading "PLAYING CARDS" over a ghosted "U.S" monogram.
  - Motion/"radiance" dashes surround the spade.
  - A bottom cartouche carries the credit line.
- **Drifters, other aces:** each is an oversized pip with an ochre offset keyline and a white ornamental knockout inside (flowers, fleur, heart-scroll) [seen].

**Jokers**
- **Monarchs:** a single **monoline gold figure**, a crowned gentleman on a penny-farthing. Wheel spokes and striped trousers are rendered as parallel lines. Below: a thin horizontal rule, a slab caption "THE JOKER", then a tiny "— MADE IN THE — / U.S.A." [seen].
- **Drifters:** a circular **sawtooth-edged medallion**, like a bottle cap or seal. Inside is a night scene: stars, a crescent, and a rider on a cliff road reading "BUCK". Wavy motion lines surround it, with a curled ribbon "BORN TO ROAM" below. Two inks (dark brown and ochre) plus paper [seen].

## 8. Rules to evoke this style (testable)

1. **Monoline.** Ornament strokes on the back and tuck use a single weight: **0.5 pt** at poker size, allowed range 0.45–0.6 pt. Test: every stroke-width in the SVG ∈ {0.5}. Frames alone may use 1.0 pt.
2. **No swelling lines.** No tapered or engraved strokes, no cross-hatching, no halftones or gradients in the art. Foil gradients exist only in the preview render.
3. **Half-hatching is the tone device.** Leaves, petals, blades and ribbon folds are split along an axis, and **exactly one half** is filled with straight parallel lines. Gap ≈2.3× stroke, pitch ≈3.3× stroke (≈0.45 mm gap at 0.5 pt). Hatch ends butt exactly onto the contour.
4. **Geometric construction only.** Build everything from arcs, circles and straight lines. Leaves are vesicas (two arcs) with a midrib. Scrolls are circular arcs. No freehand wobble. Test: paths are arcs, lines and circles, or smooth cubic approximations of them.
5. **The back is two-way by 180° rotation.** The central emblem must be identical under a 180° rotation and should *not* be a simple mirror, so that it keeps a sense of movement. The frame is mirror-symmetric on both axes.
6. **Reverse-out backs.** The back is one flood ink with art knocked out to paper. Knockout coverage (lines plus dots) is **18–22 %** of the flood area; measured values are 19–20 %.
7. **White border.** Leave **0.125 in ±0.01 (≈3.2 mm)** of unprinted paper around the flood. The flood's corner radius is **≈1.5 mm**, tighter than the card's 3.2 mm radius.
8. **Nested Deco frame.** Build it as: outer rule → stepped corners (3 steps) with dotted-square beading → a text band (motto) between double rules → an inner cartouche with concave quarter-round corners. Add piano-key bar clusters on the vertical axis and sunburst half-fans at the long-side midpoints.
9. **Circle terminals.** Free line ends on emblems (sword guards, arrow nocks, spokes, frame corner tabs) end in a small circle ≈3× stroke diameter.
10. **Interlace by gaps.** Where one element passes under another, break the under-stroke with a gap of 1.5–2× stroke on each side. Never let two strokes overlap as a plain X.
11. **Band patterns.** Bodies of snakes, rivers and ribbons are two parallel contours filled with one repeated band pattern: sawtooth triangles, diamond chain or ladder rungs.
12. **Text as ornament.** Set mottos in widely tracked (+150 to +250) all-caps condensed sans at ≈4–5 pt, flanked by em-dash rules. Words in ribbons are limited to 1–2. Use Latin or aphoristic lines. No body copy on backs.
13. **Ink budget.**
    - Back: 1 ink + paper.
    - Faces: at most 3 inks + 1 metallic (dark, red, gold metallic; navy optional).
    - Tuck: dark stock + **1 foil** + blind emboss.
    - Never more than one foil colour.
14. **Gold is line, not fill.** Foil and metallic ink render linework, crests and type. They never flood a large area. Small solid dots and squares are the only exception.
15. **Emboss selectively.** Raise only the wordmark band, crest/shield, seals and the central emblem. The ground stays flat.
16. **Ace of Spades.** Use a large solid spade (≈45 % of card width) with a monoline gold emblem inside and a 3–4-line centred gold small-caps legend below. The last line of the legend sits on an arched baseline.
17. **Joker.** One monoline figure or vignette in gold (or 2 inks), a thin horizontal rule and a wide-tracked caption. The index is "JOKER" stacked vertically in the index face.
18. **Index legibility first.** Rank cap height is **0.30–0.35 in**, the pip is ≈0.25 in, and the inset is ≥0.12 in from the trim. Use a single index family: a monoline slab (Monarchs mode) or a condensed grotesque (Drifters mode). Keep index and pip size identical across all 54 cards.
19. **Courts.** Either (a) standard two-headed pattern redrawn and recoloured, the conservative Monarchs route, or (b) custom 180°-rotational half-figures inside a thin rule frame with notched dotted corners. The latter uses a 2:1 contour-to-interior weight, flat fills only, and garment patterns built from suit pips, dots and simple repeats.
20. **Deco iconography, not Rococo.** Draw from: shields, arrows, ribbons, coin seals, sunburst rays, laurel/olive sprigs, crowns, swords, snakes, stars and crescents. Avoid acanthus, putti, cartouches with C-scrolls and bank-note guilloche.
21. **Breathing density.** No gap between parallel strokes may be smaller than 2× stroke (0.35 mm at 0.5 pt). Keep ≥1 leaf-width of flat ground between distinct motifs, and keep the emblem at ≈70–80 % of the cartouche width.
22. **Wordmark.** Use a condensed, chamfered or octagonal sans in caps, drawn as inline + offset drop shadow, on a banner. Pair it with a small secondary line in bold condensed caps and at most one tiny script accent word.
23. **Tuck composition.** The tuck is bilateral about the vertical axis, with **one** dynamic element: a diagonal banner of 6–10°, or a central roundel. The back of the tuck repeats the card-back art in foil. The seal has a die-cut perforated or scalloped edge and carries a crown or emblem and a motto.

## 9. Production specs (poker size)

- **Trim:** 2.5 × 3.5 in = **63.5 × 88.9 mm** [read: MPC, standard].
- **Bleed:** **0.125 in (3.175 mm)** per side, giving a 2.75 × 3.75 in artboard (825 × 1125 px at 300 dpi). MPC's minimum is **822 × 1122 px** at 300 dpi [read: makeplayingcards.com].
- **Safe zone:** at least **0.125 in (36 px @300 dpi)** inside the trim [read: MPC]. Keep indices ≥0.12 in from the trim; that matches the measured insets.
- **Corner radius:** industry standard **≈1/8 in (3.2 mm)**; 3.5 mm is also commonly cited [read]. The Monarchs scan measures ≈0.12 in (≈3 mm) [measured]. USPCC does not publish artwork specs publicly. Expert Playing Card Co's spec page could not be fetched (404). Use 1/8 in for USPCC-style output.
- **Back border:** flood inset 0.125 in from the trim, with the flood corner radius ≈1.5 mm (Monarchs-measured). The flood itself must not bleed if the white border is wanted.
- **Colour and resolution:** CMYK or spot, ≥300 dpi for raster [read]. Keep all art vector.
- **Foil and letterpress** [inferred]:
  - Keep foil strokes ≥0.35 pt and gaps ≥0.5 pt. The 0.5 pt monoline plus ≈1.1 pt gaps is foil-safe.
  - For letterpress, avoid solid areas larger than ~1 in², or accept speckle; Drifters embraces it.
- **Stocks:** MPC options include S30/S33 smooth and M30/M31/M32 linen/"casino/magic", with gold, silver or holographic foil and cold/hot foil stamping [read]. USPCC's "Q1" plus air-cushion finish is the Monarchs benchmark [read].
- **Tuck:** a poker tuck front is ≈2.5625 × 3.5625 in (≈65 × 90.5 mm), depth ≈0.75 in (19 mm) for 54–56 cards [read: general guidance; the Monarchs box photo ratio of 1.388 matches]. Get the actual dieline from the printer (MPC and others provide them).

## 10. Do NOT copy (signature Jinkins elements)

The goal is the sensibility. Do not reproduce any of the following, even loosely:
- two snakes interlaced around a vertical sword with leafy branches
- the Latin motto "De duobus malis…" and the "REGIS" banners
- a crown inside the spade on the Ace
- a penny-farthing joker
- the Monarchs box composition (diagonal banner, arrow-shield "EXCELSIOR", 909/Q1 coins, "Eximius")
- the "ESTO PERPETUE" crown seal
- a figure-8 snake with skulls and corner flowers on a stipple ground
- the rider-in-sawtooth-medallion joker and "BORN TO ROAM"
- the revolvers-and-skull Ace
- the rope-bordered DRIFTERS roundel tuck
- "ALL ROADS LEAD HOME"

**Translation hints for San Marcos** [inferred; verify locally]: substitute the snake-and-sword axis with a San Marcos-native vertical motif. Options:
- the spring-fed river as a sawtooth- or wave-banded ribbon
- endemic **Texas wild-rice** (a natural vesica-leaf fit for rule 3)
- the San Marcos salamander or fountain darter (instead of snakes)
- bald cypress
- Spring Lake and its springs as a sunburst
- Old Main's gables for Deco stepped frames

Write original Latin or local-language mottos. Monarchs' literary register can be kept (for example, a line about springs or perpetual flow), but the words must be new.
