## 0. The decision

### 0.1 Concept scores (1–10 per criterion)

| Criterion | A · The Spring Court | B · Strata | C · Endemics |
|---|---|---|---|
| Fidelity to the Jinkins premium line-art sensibility | 8 | 8 | 7 |
| San Marcos authenticity and depth | 9 | 8 | 7 |
| Tasteful restraint (no kitsch or souvenir clichés) | 7 | 6 | 6 |
| Playability and legibility | 7 | 7 | 7 |
| Feasibility as symmetric SVG line art | 7 | 7 | 6 |
| Internal cohesion | 9 | 7 | 8 |
| **Total (/60)** | **47** | **43** | **41** |

- **A** has the best story architecture: four houses, the St. Mark lion, and "the lower head is the reflection in the spring". Every court wears a hallmark. Its weaknesses:
  - The name "Spring Court" is already a major fandom term from *A Court of Thorns and Roses*.
  - The Cinzel index is horizontally scaled to 80 % and needs Q and J surgery.
  - It has a few whimsical notes: bubble-finial crowns and Cinzel Decorative swashes.
- **B** has the strongest museum-plate ornament: the conduit, bedding bands and sagging arc legend. But its eras pull the courts into Western costume (branding irons, lariats, cattle queens) and mix registers (a colonial alcalde beside a scuba diver). Copper also breaks from Jinkins' gold.
- **C** has the best heraldic system: a partition line per suit and a rank-graded divider. It also has the best jokers and seal. But "Endemics" over-promises, because half its courts are generic Texas (bluebonnet, pecan, monarch butterfly, bobcat). Its bird- and fish-faced royals risk whimsy, the monarch butterfly nods at the reference deck, and the bobcat sits beside the university mark.

**Director's overrides**

- **The Water Safari, Ralph and Aquarena are never named.**

### 0.3 Contradictions resolved

1. **"Engraved filigree" (the task) versus "monoline" (style.md §0).** Monoline wins. The engraved richness comes from density, half-hatching, interlace, tiny tracked type and metallic gold. There are no swelling burin lines, no cross-hatching, no guilloche, no stipple fields and no gradients in the art.
2. **Gold versus copper.** Use gold only: one metallic on the faces and one foil on the tuck. The courthouse's copper dome is drawn in gold.
3. **Index face.** All three concepts chose Cinzel at 80 % horizontal scale.
   - Scaling distorts stroke contrast.
   - The "10" crosses the safe line.
   - The Q tail collides with the pip and the J descends. All three were verified in a mock.
   - Rule 18 of style.md asks for a monoline slab or a condensed grotesque.
   - The condensed index also buys a **494 px court frame** instead of 442 px. See §D.
4. **Diagonal dividers (A) versus horizontal (B, C).** All dividers are horizontal. This is more drawable, reads cleanly as C2, and lets the house pattern and rank grade carry the variety.
5. **Stock colour.** Limestone ivory is the design ground. If only white stock is available, print on white with **no tint plate**. Every piece must pass review on both `#F4EFE3` and `#FFFFFF`.
6. **Lion overuse.** The full lion appears only on the A♠ (in moleca, frontal) and the tuck front (andante, profile). A simplified **Lion Mark** of ≤24 strokes appears as a small hallmark on every court and on the J♦ banner. It never appears on the back and never on a joker.

## A. Identity

**Deck name:** **HEADWATERS**
- The San Marcos River's headwaters are Spring Lake.
- The name is a plural noun like MONARCHS and DRIFTERS.
- Searched 2026-09-22: no playing-card deck by this name was found. "Wellspring" is a Magic: The Gathering card name. **Clear the trademark before print.**

**Subline:** PLAYING CARDS OF THE SAN MARCOS SPRINGS
**Tagline:** *Long may it flow.*
**Mottos** (original Latin, max. one per piece):
- **NUMQUAM DEFICIT** ("it never fails"): tuck ribbon.
- **NUSQUAM ALIBI** ("nowhere else"): seal.

**Houses (suits)**

| Suit | House | Theme |
|---|---|---|
| ♠ | **the Deep** | The Edwards Aquifer, limestone, the Balcones fault and the blind salamander |
| ♥ | **the Fount** | Spring Lake's surface: the vents, glass-bottom boats, Aquamaid heritage and song |
| ♣ | **the Reed** | The riverbanks: Texas wild-rice, bald cypress, heron and kingfisher, pecan |

**Narrative (118 words)**

> Where the Balcones fault splits Hill Country from plain, the Edwards Aquifer rises through more than two hundred spring openings, seventy-two degrees every day of the year and never known to cease. People have gathered at these springs for twelve thousand years. The name San Marcos was first given in 1689, for St. Mark, and his winged lion keeps its imagined court. HEADWATERS deals that court in four houses. The Deep (♠) keeps the aquifer's dark halls. The Fount (♥) holds the wonder of Spring Lake. The Reed (♣) tends a wild-rice found nowhere else. The Ford (♦) guards the old road's river crossing. Every royal wears two faces: the figure and its reflection. Long may it flow.

### B.1 Artboard

| Item | Value |
|---|---|
| viewBox | `0 0 750 1050` (trim; poker 2.5 × 3.5 in at 300 ppi) |
| Card corner radius | **37.5** (1/8 in) |

| Seal | Round, Ø **330** (1.1 in). |

### B.2 Line-weight system (the only legal stroke widths)

| Token | px | pt | Where it is used |
|---|---|---|---|
| **HAIRLINE** | **1.6** | 0.38 | Caption em-rules on small type, spikelet awns, dashed spandrel contours on the back. Never a contour. This is the foil minimum; nothing is thinner. |
| **FINE** | **2.1** | 0.5 | **The monoline.** All ornament on the back, tuck and seal; ace emblems; joker detail; court garment patterns; **all hatching**; the gold inner frame rule; divider-band rules. |
| **MEDIUM** | **3.1** | 0.75 | Court interior detail (features, seams, fingers, attribute detail), joker contours, the house partition line in the divider, and knockout lines inside solid pips. |
| **RULE** | **4.2** | 1.0 | The court frame, the back's outer rule, the tuck's outer rule, and court upper eyelids. |
| **CONTOUR** | **6.25** | 1.5 | Court figure silhouettes and major attribute silhouettes only. This gives a 2 : 1 ratio with MEDIUM. |

**Hatching**
- Lines are FINE at a **7.0 px pitch** (4.9 px gap).
- Exactly **one half** of a split shape is hatched.
- Angles:
  - 45° on blades, strata and panels
  - perpendicular to the midrib on leaves
  - 45° on the wordmark shadow
- No cross-hatching.

**Dots and terminals**
- Dots are Ø 4.2, 6.3 or 8.4.
- The circle terminal on free line ends is **Ø 6.3**.
- At an interlace crossing, the under-stroke breaks with a **4.2 px gap each side**.

**Caps and joins**
- **Ornament and figure strokes:** `stroke-linecap: round`, `stroke-linejoin: round`.
- **Hatch lines:** `butt` caps. They are clipped to the region and meet the contour's centreline, and the contour stroke covers the join. There is no overshoot.
- **Frames, rules, fault-steps, lozenges, ashlar and arcades:** `miter` joins (miterlimit 4) and `butt` caps.
- **Vesica leaves, spikelets, stalactite points and compass points:** `miter` joins with **miterlimit 10**, so the tips stay sharp.

**Scaling**
- Never let a transform scale a stroke. Author strokes at final size, or divide the width by the symbol's scale factor.
- No `vector-effect`, no filters and no blend modes.

**Colour rules**
1. **Gold is line and regalia.**
   - Flat gold fills are allowed only on crowns, orbs, sceptres, keys, instruments, clasps and hair locks.
   - Total gold must be **≤ 15 % of the court art-window area**.
   - Gold is never a flood on the back, tuck or seal.
2. **Court colour balance target:** paper 45–50 %, jade 15–20 %, red 12–16 %, gold 10–15 %, Aquifer line and small accents ≈ 12 %.
3. **Solid Aquifer fills** only on pips, the A♠/A♣ silhouettes, pupils, dots, the grackle body, and accents ≤ 8 % of a court.
4. **Patterns on fills** are drawn in Aquifer (on jade, gold or paper) or knocked out to paper (on red or jade).
   - Gold may sit on a red field only as a **solid shape with an Aquifer contour**: a rowel star, a bead, a buckle.
   - **Thin gold linework on red is never allowed**, because metallic on red goes muddy. This also applies to red ace pips, whose interiors are paper knockouts.

There are **two families only.** All type is outlined with `inkkit.typeset.text_to_path`; no `<text>` appears in the art. - **Tuck front:** cap 60 px on a gentle convex arc (a "gateway lintel", radius 1300 px).
- **A♠:** cap 30 px, straight, no inline and no shadow.

## E. Pips and pip-card layout

### E.1 Silhouettes

Silhouettes are standard, refined, and built only from circles and straight lines. Pips are **solid suit ink with no internal ornament** everywhere except the aces. Build each once as a `<symbol>`/path and reuse it; never redraw. The unit **u = the spade's width**.

**Heart (1.00 u × 0.92 u)**
- The lobes are circles of r = 0.26 u centred at (±0.24 u, 0.26 u).
- The sides are straight tangents from each circle to the point at (0, 0.92 u).
- The top cleft is sharp, 0.16 u deep.
- **Optical size:** draw hearts at **1.04 ×** the unit.

**Spade (1.00 u × 1.10 u)**
- **Body:** the heart construction flipped (point up) and scaled to 0.80 u tall, with the apex at the top.
- **Stem:** a straight-sided trapezoid from 0.07 u wide (at y 0.62 u, hidden in the lobes) to 0.20 u wide at y 1.00 u.
- **Foot:** the **fault-step plinth**, the deck's one signature pip detail (the Balcones step). The upper step is 0.26 u × 0.05 u; the lower step is 0.36 u × 0.05 u, bottoming at 1.10 u.

**Club (1.04 u × 1.04 u)**
- Three circles of r = 0.245 u at (0, 0.245 u) and (±0.275 u, 0.53 u).
- A filled triangular core joins them.
- The stem is the same taper, 0.07 u → 0.20 u, from y 0.55 u to 0.94 u.
- The same fault-step plinth runs to 1.04 u.

**Diamond (0.80 u × 1.12 u)**
- A rhombus whose four sides bow **inward** by a circular sagitta of **3 %** of the side length, like a cut ford-stone.
- The tips stay sharp.

## G. Ornament vocabulary

All motifs are monoline FINE unless noted, and built from circles, arcs and straight lines. Here "half-hatched" always means FINE at a 7.0 pitch in one half only.

1. **Source Rosette** (back centre; a reduced version on the tuck, A♥ and K♠ orb). Concentric from the centre, radii for the back:
   - a crater r 20 with 8 curved ribs;
   - ripple rings at r 30, 39, 50 (×1.28 steps);
   - a **bubble ring** at r 62 (24 circles Ø 7);
   - a **pinwheel band** from r 72 to 104: 24 ribs, each a circular arc twisted 25° clockwise, with alternate cells half-hatched;
   - a **running-wave ring** from r 110 to 124, flowing clockwise;
   - an outer rule at r 130.

   It is C_N, so C2-safe. Where D2 is needed, use straight ribs (no twist).
2. **Lion Mark (in moleca).** Frontal, never with a halo, book or inscription.
   - **Head:** a face circle with almond eyes, a trapezoid nose and a closed mouth.
   - **Mane:** 3 rings of scalloped arcs, 12 outer scallops.
   - **Wings:** two sickle arcs rising from behind the mane and curling inward over the head. Each wing has 3 rows of scalloped coverts and 5 vesica primaries, one half of each hatched.
   - **Paws:** two forepaws resting on a ripple line.
   - **Simplified Lion Mark** (≤ 60 px): face circle, 12-scallop mane ring, two wing arcs with 3 primaries each, and a ripple line, in ≤ 24 strokes. This is the court hallmark.
3. **Lion andante** (tuck front only).
   - A profile walking to the viewer's left, head in profile.
   - **Forepaws on a 3-course limestone ledge** (half-hatched) and **hind paws in 3 ripple lines**: the Balcones image.
   - Upswept wings: scalloped coverts plus 5 vesica primaries.
   - The tail curls up into a tuft ending in a circle terminal.
   - Mane in scalloped arcs.
4. **Wild-rice ribbon leaf.**
   - The midrib is an S made of two tangent arcs.
   - The outline is a vesica-width profile with length : width 10–14 : 1.
   - It is split along the midrib; **one side is hatched perpendicular to the midrib**.
5. **Wild-rice flowering stalk** (botanically true along the stalk's own axis).
   - **Erect female spikelets toward the tip:** 3 : 1 vesicas on 6 px pedicels at ±15°, each with one HAIRLINE awn.
   - **Drooping male florets below them:** the same vesicas hung at ±150°.
   - In two-way (rotated) copies the whole stalk inverts. That is acceptable.
6. **Wild-rice wreath.**
   - An arc stem with paired ribbon-leaf vesicas every 14°, shrinking 6 % per pair toward the tips.
   - Its ends are tied with a reed-node knot.
   - It replaces classical laurel. There is no laurel or olive in this deck.
7. **Running-wave scroll** (Vitruvian wave). A repeated quarter-circle rising into a semicircle curl between two rules, at a 30 px pitch and 10 px amplitude. On D2 frames it is mirrored at the axis and flows **outward**; on rings it flows clockwise.
8. **Ripple rings.** Groups of 3 concentric circles, ellipses or arcs, with gaps growing ×1.3 outward.
9. **Bubble beading.** A row of circles growing ×1.2 in the **direction of rise**. Ø 3–8 px on the back and tuck, up to 12 px on the A♠. It replaces dotted-square beading.
10. **Fault-step.** A line broken by one right-angle step 4–5× the stroke tall. Used in the ♠ divider, K♠ crown, pip plinth and back plinths. **Never a triple stair with dot beading.**
11. **Strata bands.** Horizontal courses alternating 12 and 19 px tall, with every other course hatched at 45°. An optional single diagonal fault jog shifts the courses one course-height.
12. **Karst voids.** A staggered grid of outlined ellipses in three sizes (Ø 6 / 10 / 16). The largest carries a FINE inner offset.
13. **Stalactite point.** A tall isosceles kite with 3 growth-ring arcs across it, one half hatched. Used in diadems and drip fringes.
14. **Drip fringe.** Vertical strokes whose lengths follow a slow sine envelope, each ending in a Ø 6.3 terminal.
15. **Gill plume.** A curved spine with 6–8 barbs on one side, shortening toward the tip, which ends in a Ø 4.2 dot. Printed in Gill Red on faces.
16. **Scale lattice.** Rows of semicircular arcs, each row offset by half a pitch and stepped by 0.6 r, clipped to the shape. Used only on Q♥'s bodice and K♥'s trim.
17. **Cypress-knee crenellation.** Rounded conical knobs, each two arcs meeting in a small top fillet, at heights 1.0 / 0.8 / 0.65.
18. **Comb spray** (cypress foliage). A straight or arc rachis with evenly spaced short parallel ticks on both sides. Tick lengths follow a vesica envelope. The end may carry a round cone (a circle with 4 scale arcs).
19. **Reed ladder.** Two rules 16 px apart with rungs every 26 px and a node ellipse on every 4th rung.
20. **Ashlar courses.** Running-bond rectangles with 3 px chamfered corners (rusticated limestone).
21. **Arcade.** Round arches, each a semicircle on two short jambs with a keystone wedge (the courthouse windows).
22. **Rowel star.** An 8-point star wheel around a hub circle. The points are split lozenges with one half hatched.
23. **Stepping-stone chain.** Lozenges spaced evenly between a double rule, with a larger centre stone.
24. **Current lines** (hair, beards, plumes). 3–5 parallel offset curves at a 7 px pitch from one guide, each ending in a Ø 6.3 terminal. Alternate locks may be flat gold.
25. **Fountain darter.**
    - A slender fish (length : depth 6 : 1) with a fanned first dorsal fin (9 spines with scalloped membrane arcs).
    - A **stitch line** of 7 px dashes with 4 px gaps along the flank.
    - 8 short saddle bars across the back.
    - A round eye with a dot.
26. **Spring Lake lens.** A vertical vesica cartouche made of two circular arcs.
    - **Lens offset field:** 4–7 outward offset contours of the lens at an 11 px pitch, HAIRLINE. They turn to dashes (8 on, 6 off) progressively toward the corners, like rings spreading across the lake.
27. **Vent roundel** (frame corners). An outer circle, an inner circle of Ø 16, and 12 **straight** radial ribs between them (achiral, D2-safe).
28. **Festoon catenary.** Catenary arcs with Ø 5–6 bulbs at the low point and at the joins (downtown string lights).
29. **Pearl beading.** Graduated circles along a line or arc, largest at the centre.
30. **Conduit.** Two parallel FINE lines 12–18 px apart carrying rising bubbles. Anything it crosses breaks with a 4 px gap.
31. **Tooled scroll** (vaquero leather tooling). A volute of tangent circular arcs with one vesica leaf at each turn. Used **only** on ♦ cuffs and belts. It replaces acanthus.

**Banned vocabulary:** acanthus, putti, C-scroll cartouches, guilloche, stepped Deco corners with dotted-square beading, piano-key bars, sunburst half-fans on the long sides, concave quarter-round cartouche corners, rope borders, stipple-dot grounds, sawtooth medallions, crossed weapons or arrows in shields.

## I. Review checklist (answer yes/no; every item must be **yes**)

1. **Stroke widths:** every stroke width, after transforms, is in {1.6, 2.1, 3.1, 4.2, 6.25}, and no transform scales a stroke.
2. **No banned techniques:** no tapered or variable strokes, cross-hatching, guilloche, stipple fields, gradients, filters or blend modes. Gradients appear only in tuck and seal previews.
3. **Tone:** tone comes only from half-hatching at a 7.0 pitch. Hatch lines butt onto contours without overshoot.
4. **Palette:** every fill and stroke is a §C token, and each piece stays within its §C ink budget.
5. **Gold:** used only as line or regalia, ≤ 15 % of the court art window, never a flood on the back or tuck. There is no thin gold linework on red; gold on red appears only as contoured solid shapes.
6. **Index:** identical font, size and position on all 54 cards. An overlay diff of the top-left 140 × 240 region differs only in glyph and colour, and no rank moves by more than 1 px.
7. **Legibility at 25 %:** rendered 188 px wide, the rank, suit and court rank of every card are identifiable at a glance.
8. **Safe zone:** no type or critical art lies outside the 37.5 px inset (the back's flood edge excepted).
9. **Pip cards:** layouts match §E.2 exactly, and every pip below y = 525 is rotated.
10. **Courts:**
    - (a) The render equals its own 180° rotation.
    - (b) The frame, band and pip positions match §F.1.
    - (c) The figure keeps ≥ 12 px clear of the corner pip.
11. **Card back:**
    - (a) The frame is mirror-symmetric on both axes.
    - (b) The emblem is identical under 180° rotation but **not** under a mirror.
    - (c) Coverage is 18–22 %.
12. **Spacing:** the gap between parallel strokes is ≥ 4.2 px. Knockout lines inside solid shapes are ≥ 2.5 px, with gaps ≥ 3 px.
13. **Interlace:** every crossing is gapped (4.2 px each side). No plain X overlaps.
14. **Line ends:** free line ends finish in Ø 6.3 circle terminals or bubbles where specified. No ragged ends.
15. **Face kit:** all 12 faces follow §H.0, with ≤ 14 strokes, paper skin and the eye construction. They look drawn by one hand.
16. **Gaze and attributes:** the gaze plan and one-eyed cards (J♠, J♥, K♦) are correct. Tall attributes sit on the viewer's right.
17. **Type:**
    - only Barlow Condensed and Roboto Slab;
    - everything outlined;
    - caps tracked as specified;
    - copy matches §J.2 **verbatim**, with Latin and spelling checked.
18. **Style fidelity:** the piece reads as Jinkins-grade monoline Americana (density, half-hatching, restraint), not as Victorian bank-note engraving, fantasy art or clip-art.
19. **No Jinkins signatures:** none of the elements in style.md §10, §J.1 or the §G banned list, even loosely. That includes notched or dotted frame corners, and a crown inside the spade.
20. **Restraint:** no souvenir clichés, namely:
    - cowboy hats or six-shooters;
    - cacti, longhorn skulls or bluebonnet fields;
    - the Texas outline or Lone Star flag;
    - shell bras or mermaid tails;
    - inner tubes or beer.
21. **IP:** no university names or marks, bobcats or maroon-and-gold pairings. No city, county, Aquarena, Meadows Center, Mermaid Society, venue or Lions Club marks.
22. **Sensitivity:**
    - no real or historical persons;
    - no Indigenous people, regalia, patterns or language;
    - no halo, book or inscription on the lion, and no lion on the jokers;
    - no mill wheel or flood imagery.
23. **Species:** accurate (salamander gills and eye-dots, darter stitch line, wild-rice florets), and never handled by a figure.
24. **Stock:** the art works on both Limestone `#F4EFE3` and white `#FFFFFF`.
25. **Rendering:** the file renders identically in `rsvg-convert` and `resvg`. Colour groups are separated with the ids `paper`, `jade`, `red`, `gold`, `ink`, and no `<text>` elements remain.
